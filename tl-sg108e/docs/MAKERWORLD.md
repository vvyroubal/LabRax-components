## Description

**A 1U rack panel for the TP-Link TL-SG108E Easy Smart switch, for a Lab Rax 10 inch rack.**

The switch slides in from the front through a 159 x 26 mm opening and stands
in it with its face flush with the panel. Nothing covers its ports or LEDs.
Behind the panel an arm runs along each side of the switch: a channel whose
lips reach over its top and bottom edges, with a stop at the back so pushing
a plug in cannot push the switch back. A sprung finger on each side presses
on its top and holds it in place.

It bolts to the front posts only. The switch weighs about 250 g, so it needs
nothing at the back.

The rack itself is not included. It is the Lab Rax bolted 5U rack:
https://makerworld.com/en/models/1464819-lab-rax-10-server-rack-bolted-version-5u
and the rest of the system is in the Lab Rax collection:
https://makerworld.com/en/collections/5813742-lab-rax

**The device**

TP-Link TL-SG108E Easy Smart switch, 158 x 101 x 25 mm. Ports and LEDs at the
front, power jack at the back. The panel is open behind the switch.

**What is in the file**

Two plates, already arranged for a Bambu Lab A1 mini (180 x 180 mm bed):

- Plate 1: the middle of the panel, face down and turned 45 degrees: the
  plate round the opening, both arms, the fingers and the stops
- Plate 2: the two ears, standing on their bottom edges

A 254 mm panel will not fit a 180 mm bed any way round, so the ears are
separate. Nothing is overhung past 45 degrees, so no support is needed.

**Printing**

- Material: PETG. PLA will work in a cool room.
- Profile: 0.20 mm Standard for the A1 mini, as carried in the file. 2 walls,
  15% infill.
- Filament: about 83 g for both plates.
- Time: about 4 h 15 min for both plates.

**Hardware to buy**

Every screw is the same: M6 x 12 button head.

- 10 x M6 x 12 button head screws
- 6 x M6 washers (12.5 mm outside diameter), for the rack
- 4 x M6 nuts (10 mm across the flats), for the ears

The rack's posts already hold the nuts for the six rack screws. The washers
are needed: those screws go through slots, and a bare screw head will sink
into the plastic.

**Assembly**

1. Slide an M6 nut into each of the two slots in each ear's tongue, from the
   tongue's inner end, flats top and bottom.
2. Put each ear's tongue in behind the end of the middle's plate, up against
   the arm, and drive two M6 x 12 from the front through the plate into the
   nuts.
3. Bolt the panel to the front posts with six M6 x 12 and washers.
4. Slide the switch in through the opening until it stops.

To take the switch out, pull it forward by its sides.

**Good to know**

- Every joint screw goes in from the front. Nothing stands out at the sides
  behind the ears, so the panel goes into the rack without catching on the
  posts.
- If the switch is loose, the fingers' grip is `PNL_GRIP` in the source, 1 mm.
  If it is tight in the channels, raise `PNL_CLR` from 0.5.
- Case dimensions are TP-Link's published figures. Measure your unit before
  printing.
